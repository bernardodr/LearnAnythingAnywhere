# Release notes

What's new in each release of Learn Anything Anywhere, newest first. Each feature links to the part of the guide that explains how to use it.

## 0.18: Sources for every lesson
*26 November 2026*

### New

- **Every lesson ends with its sources.** Before a lesson is written, LAA searches the web for two to four real pages about what it teaches, from encyclopedias, museums, universities, public bodies and respected publishers, and opens each one to check it's really there and really about what it says. Only those that pass are shown, numbered at the end of the lesson and in its text. [Sources →](guide/04-reading-a-lesson.md#sources)
- **Your book's sources fill up as you learn.** The sources found for each lesson join *Sources* at the back of the book, alongside any you add yourself. [D. Sources →](guide/03-your-contents-page.md#d-sources)

### Good to know

- **Nothing is ever made up.** A lesson only shows sources a search really found and LAA checked, or ones you added. If none can be checked, the lesson says so.
- **For children,** with child-safe mode on, LAA only searches, and only keeps, well-known educational sites.

## 0.17: The reading ribbon, and no more em dashes
*26 November 2026*

### New

- **The reading ribbon.** The book you read last wears a red ribbon bookmark, with *Continue reading* under it in your library, so you can pick up where you left off. It moves to whichever book you open a lesson in next. [Your library →](guide/01-getting-started.md#your-library)

### Changed

- **No more em dashes.** Everything you read in LAA, from your lessons and diagrams to your tutor's replies and your mission, is now written without those long dashes: you'll see commas, colons and full stops instead. That includes lessons you already have.
- **A book still in its preface** now says *Preface in progress* under it, without a ribbon: the ribbon is for the book you're reading.

## 0.16: Leather and linen, and more hands-on diagrams
*26 November 2026*

### New

- **Books bound in leather and linen.** Forest green and terracotta books are leather, with a fine pebbled grain; ochre and dusty blue books are linen, with its slubby weave. Each book has its own cut of the material, on its cover and its spine. [Your library →](guide/01-getting-started.md#your-library)
- **More hands-on diagrams.** New diagrams let you drag things and watch the picture answer, make a prediction and then see what happens, or build a picture up one layer at a time. [Interactive diagrams →](guide/04-reading-a-lesson.md#interactive-diagrams)
- **A menu on each book's page.** **•••** at the top right moves the book earlier or later in your library, without dragging. [Your book's page →](guide/03-your-contents-page.md)

### Changed

- **No more Arrange button.** To arrange your library, drag a book to its place (on a phone, press and hold it first), or use the book's **•••** menu. [Arranging your library →](guide/01-getting-started.md#arranging-your-library)

### Fixed

- **Diagram buttons are always readable.** A chosen button in a diagram could have pale text on a pale background. LAA now checks every word in a diagram, and turns any hard-to-read text dark or light to suit, in diagrams you already have too.

- **The preface opens like a book,** with *Before we begin* and a small ornament. Your answers sit like notes in the margin, and **Send** waits at the bottom of the screen. [The preface →](guide/02-starting-a-textbook.md#3-the-preface-why-do-you-want-to-learn-this)

- **Back to your library** is now an arrow at the top left of each of these screens.

## 0.8: Email, limits and fixes
*16 November 2026*

LAA now sends email: to confirm your address, and to reset a forgotten password. It also caps how much AI can be used each day, and a few problems are fixed.

### New

- **Confirm your email.** A new account gets an email with a link. Until you open it, you can look around, but starting a textbook waits. Lost it? Tap **Send the link again** on your shelf. Children count as confirmed once their parent has confirmed. [Creating your account →](guide/01-getting-started.md#creating-your-account)
- **Forgotten your password?** Ask for a reset link on the title page, then choose a new password from the email. [Forgotten your password? →](guide/01-getting-started.md#forgotten-your-password)
- **A daily allowance.** Each learner can use AI up to a daily limit, and so can LAA as a whole; both start again at midnight UTC. [Daily allowance →](guide/06-good-to-know.md#daily-allowance)

### Fixed

- Opening LAA for the first time could occasionally lose the reader settings you saved straight away. Every learner now has exactly one set of settings.
- The safety check is harder to trick: text that imitates the check's own markings is treated as plain text, and a part it flags stays flagged.
- Very large requests are refused.

### Good to know

- **Practice mode** only appears on test setups now. A real copy of LAA always uses a real AI service.
- A few new accounts can be made from one network every 15 minutes, and a few emails sent to one address.

## 0.7: Passwords
*16 November 2026*

LAA now has proper accounts: you sign in with your email and a password, and nobody can open your shelf just by typing your email.

### New

- **Create an account** with your email and a password, as a learner or a parent. [Creating your account →](guide/01-getting-started.md#creating-your-account)
- **Sign in with your password.** A wrong email or password gets the same message, and after 5 wrong tries signing in pauses for 15 minutes, so passwords can't be guessed. [Signing in →](guide/01-getting-started.md#signing-in)
- **Long, simple passwords.** At least 15 characters, with no rules about symbols or numbers: three or four random words work well. Common passwords are turned down, with a reason. **Show** lets you check what you typed. [Choosing a password →](guide/01-getting-started.md#choosing-a-password)
- **Change your password** in **Settings**. Changing it signs you out everywhere else. [Your password →](guide/01-getting-started.md#your-password)
- **Children's passwords.** A parent chooses their child's password when adding them, and can reset it later (with the parent PIN, once it's set). [Replaced in 0.9 →](#09-one-account-per-family)
- **Owner accounts** need an owner setup code when they're made. [Making an owner account →](guide/08-the-owner-console.md#making-an-owner-account)

### Improved

- **The parent PIN** pauses for 15 minutes after 5 wrong tries. [The parent PIN lock →](guide/07-reader-settings.md#the-parent-pin-lock)

### Good to know

- **Accounts from before 0.7** have no password. On a test setup they can still sign in without one, then set a password under **Settings → Your password**.
- **There's no reset email yet.** A parent can reset their child's password, but an adult who forgets theirs can't get back in for now.

## 0.6: Reader settings and safety
*14 November 2026*

Choose how your textbooks are written: the reading level, what's left out, whether links are allowed, and whether answers can ever be shown. Parents can add their children, set up their learning, and lock it with a PIN. And everything LAA writes, and everything typed into it, is now checked against those settings.

### New

- **Reader settings.** Tap **Settings** on your shelf: child-safe mode, an age setting that shows the reading level, *Leave out of lessons* switches plus your own topics to leave out, links (*Off*, *Allowlist* or *Open*), and *Reveal answer after 3 tries*. [Your settings →](guide/07-reader-settings.md#your-settings)
- **Children's accounts.** Parents can add a child with their own shelf, sign-in email, age and child-safe mode, and manage their settings. Children can see their settings but not change them. [Replaced in 0.9 →](#09-one-account-per-family)
- **Parent PIN lock.** Once set, changing a child's settings asks for the PIN. [The parent PIN lock →](guide/07-reader-settings.md#the-parent-pin-lock)
- **See the answer, if your settings allow it.** With *Reveal answer after 3 tries* on, once every hint is used and you've tried three times, the next tap shows *A strong answer*. [Your settings →](guide/07-reader-settings.md#your-settings)
- **Safety checks on everything.** Lessons, the tutor's replies and notes, and what you type are checked against the reader's settings. A lesson part that doesn't fit is rewritten or left out with a note; for children, the tutor's replies appear only once they've been checked. [How LAA keeps things safe →](guide/07-reader-settings.md#how-laa-keeps-things-safe)
- **Links follow your settings** everywhere, including your sources at the back of the book.
- **Owner console:** *Compare before switching* now includes the safety check. [Compare →](guide/08-the-owner-console.md#compare-before-switching)

### Improved

- Topics are now checked against the reader's settings too: a topic that touches on something left out is declined kindly, with ideas for something else.
- If the safety check can't run for a moment, nothing is saved or shown, and you're asked to try again.

### Good to know

- New accounts start with links set to *Allowlist* (only links to sources you've added), and answers are never shown until you choose otherwise.
- The guide's owner console page is now page 8; reader settings are page 7.

*14 November 2026*

The people who run LAA now have a console of their own: choose which AI service does each job, add backups, try services side by side before switching, and see every request with its cost.

### New

## 0.4: Your tutor
*14 November 2026*

Lessons now come with a personal tutor. Answer a practice question in your own words, and your tutor tells you how close you are and helps you get there, without handing you the answer. When you get it right, LAA makes a note of what you've learned, and your next lessons build on it.

### New

- **Send your practice answer to your tutor.** Every lesson's practice question is now marked by your tutor. [How it works →](guide/05-practice-and-your-tutor.md#answering-a-practice-question)
- **The tutor's note.** See at a glance whether your answer is *Met*, *Partly there* or *Not yet*, which ideas you've already got, and how many are still to find, plus a question to point you the right way. [Reading the note →](guide/05-practice-and-your-tutor.md#reading-the-tutors-note)
- **Hints, one at a time.** Up to three hints per question, from gentle to more direct, so you only take as much help as you need. [Hints →](guide/05-practice-and-your-tutor.md#improving-your-answer)
- **Revise and resend.** Improve your answer and send it again, as often as you like.
- **Your tutor never gives the answer away.** Every message is checked first. If it would give too much away, you get a prepared hint instead.
- **Chat with your tutor.** *Stuck or curious?* at the end of each lesson is now a real conversation: ask anything about the lesson, and your conversation is kept for when you come back. [Chatting with your tutor →](guide/05-practice-and-your-tutor.md#chatting-with-your-tutor)
- **What you've learned, and your glossary.** When you get a practice question fully right, a note on what you've shown you can do appears at the back of the book, and words you used correctly join your glossary. Future lessons are planned from them. [At the back of the book →](guide/03-your-contents-page.md#at-the-back-of-the-book)

### Improved

- The contents page now updates as soon as you come back to it, so new notes and finished lessons show straight away.
- Coming back to a lesson now shows your tutor's latest note and any hints you've already seen.

### Good to know

- The option to show the answer after a few tries arrives with reader settings in a later release. For now, answers are never shown.

## 0.1: First light
*2 November 2026*

The foundations: LAA's title page, and the engine that writes your textbooks.

### New

- **The title page:** *A textbook for whatever you're curious about.*
- **A choice of AI services.** LAA can have its writing done by different AI services (from OpenAI, Google or Anthropic), and the people who run it can switch between them at any time without interrupting anyone's learning. If one service has a problem, another can take over.
- **Practice mode.** LAA can run without any AI service, for trying it out and testing. [Practice mode →](guide/06-good-to-know.md#practice-mode-text-that-starts-with-fake)
