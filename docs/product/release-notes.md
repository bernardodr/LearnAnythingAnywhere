# Release notes

What's new in each release of Learn Anything Anywhere, newest first. Each feature links to the part of the guide that explains how to use it.

## 0.5: The owner console
*14 November 2026*

The people who run LAA now have a console of their own: choose which AI service does each job, add backups, try services side by side before switching, and see every request with its cost.

### New

- **AI services at a glance.** A card for each service, showing whether it's ready, which models it has, and how many jobs it's doing. Make one the default, or turn it off. [AI services →](guide/07-the-owner-console.md#ai-services)
- **Routing.** Choose the service and model size for each job (lesson writing, tutor chat, grading and the rest), plus backups to try if it fails. Choices a job can't use are refused, with the reason. [Routing →](guide/07-the-owner-console.md#routing-who-does-which-job)
- **Compare before switching.** Run the same request through two services side by side, with automatic checks: right shape, fair quizzes and complete lessons, and how easy the writing is to read. Then switch with one tap. [Compare →](guide/07-the-owner-console.md#compare-before-switching)
- **Run log.** Every request with its status, timing and rough cost, filtered by job or service. [Run log →](guide/07-the-owner-console.md#run-log)
- **"Practice mode"** is now the name for the stand-in services, everywhere.

## 0.4: Your tutor
*14 November 2026*

Lessons now come with a personal tutor. Answer a practice question in your own words, and your tutor tells you how close you are and helps you get there, without handing you the answer. When you get it right, LAA makes a note of what you've learned, and your next lessons build on it.

### New

- **Send your practice answer to your tutor.** Every lesson's practice question is now marked by your tutor. [How it works →](guide/05-practice-and-your-tutor.md#answering-a-practice-question)
- **The tutor's note.** See at a glance whether your answer is *Met*, *Partly there* or *Not yet*, which ideas you've already got, and how many are still to find, plus a question to point you the right way. [Reading the note →](guide/05-practice-and-your-tutor.md#reading-the-tutors-note)
- **Hints, one at a time.** Up to three hints per question, from gentle to more direct, so you only take as much help as you need. [Hints →](guide/05-practice-and-your-tutor.md#improving-your-answer)
- **Revise and resend.** Improve your answer and send it again, as often as you like.
- **Your tutor never gives the answer away.** Every message is checked first. If it would give too much away, you get a prepared hint instead.
- **Chat with your tutor.** *Stuck or curious?* at the end of each lesson is now a real conversation: ask anything about the lesson, and your conversation is kept for when you come back. [Chatting with your tutor →](guide/05-practice-and-your-tutor.md#chatting-with-your-tutor)
- **What you've learned, and your glossary.** When you get a practice question fully right, a note on what you've shown you can do appears at the back of the book, and words you used correctly join your glossary. Future lessons are planned from them. [At the back of the book →](guide/03-your-contents-page.md#at-the-back-of-the-book)

### Improved

- The contents page now updates as soon as you come back to it, so new notes and finished lessons show straight away.
- Coming back to a lesson now shows your tutor's latest note and any hints you've already seen.

### Good to know

- The option to show the answer after a few tries arrives with reader settings in a later release. For now, answers are never shown.

## 0.3: Lessons
*9 November 2026*

Your textbook now has real lessons. Each one is short, teaches one thing tied to your mission, and is checked before you see it.

### New

- **Your first lesson, straight away.** As soon as you confirm your mission, lesson 1 is written for you, and you can watch its progress on the contents page. [How new lessons arrive →](guide/03-your-contents-page.md#how-new-lessons-arrive)
- **The contents page.** Your lessons in order, marked *Read* or *Read next*, with **Continue reading** to pick up where you left off. [Your contents page →](guide/03-your-contents-page.md)
- **Lessons built to stick.** Each lesson has your win for the day, short sections, a key idea, a common mistake to watch out for, and steps to try for real. [The parts of a lesson →](guide/04-reading-a-lesson.md#the-parts-of-a-lesson)
- **Quizzes with instant feedback.** Warm-up and *Check yourself* questions tell you straight away whether you're right, and why. Answers are shuffled and written so the right one can't be spotted by its wording. [Quizzes →](guide/04-reading-a-lesson.md#quizzes)
- **Warm-ups.** From lesson 2 on, each lesson starts by asking you to recall something from an earlier one.
- **Cheat sheets.** Every lesson comes with a one-page summary, collected under *Reference sheets* at the back of the book. [Cheat sheet →](guide/04-reading-a-lesson.md#cheat-sheet)
- **One lesson at a time.** Tap **I've finished this lesson**, then write your next one, so each lesson can build on how you're getting on. [Finishing a lesson →](guide/04-reading-a-lesson.md#finishing-a-lesson)
- **The back of the book.** Reference sheets, glossary, what you've learned, and sources. [At the back of the book →](guide/03-your-contents-page.md#at-the-back-of-the-book)
- **Add your own sources.** LAA never makes up books or websites. Add the ones you trust, and later lessons can point to them. [Adding a source →](guide/03-your-contents-page.md#d-sources)

### Fixed

- Going back to the contents page from a lesson no longer leaves an out-of-date copy of the page behind.

## 0.2: Your library and your mission
*9 November 2026*

You can now sign in, see your shelf of textbooks, and start a new one. Every textbook begins with a short interview about *why* you want to learn the topic, which becomes your mission.

### New

- **Sign in** with your email. LAA remembers you on each device. [Signing in →](guide/01-getting-started.md#signing-in)
- **Your shelf.** Your textbooks as cloth-bound covers, each with its mission underneath, plus suggestions to get you started. [Your shelf →](guide/01-getting-started.md#your-shelf)
- **Start a textbook on anything.** Tap **+**, name a topic, and begin. [Choose a topic →](guide/02-starting-a-textbook.md#1-choose-a-topic)
- **A topic check.** Every topic is checked first. Almost all go straight ahead; a few are approached in a different way or kindly declined. [The topic check →](guide/02-starting-a-textbook.md#2-the-topic-check)
- **The preface.** A short conversation with your tutor about what you want to be able to do, with replies that appear as they're written. [The preface →](guide/02-starting-a-textbook.md#3-the-preface-why-do-you-want-to-learn-this)
- **Your mission card.** Your goal, why it matters, what success looks like, your limits, and what's out of scope. Edit anything, then tap **Write my textbook**. [Your mission card →](guide/02-starting-a-textbook.md#4-your-mission-card)

## 0.1: First light
*2 November 2026*

The foundations: LAA's title page, and the engine that writes your textbooks.

### New

- **The title page:** *A textbook for whatever you're curious about.*
- **A choice of AI services.** LAA can have its writing done by different AI services (from OpenAI, Google or Anthropic), and the people who run it can switch between them at any time without interrupting anyone's learning. If one service has a problem, another can take over.
- **Practice mode.** LAA can run without any AI service, for trying it out and testing. [Practice mode →](guide/06-good-to-know.md#practice-mode-text-that-starts-with-fake)
