## Practice mode: text that starts with "[FAKE]"

LAA can run in **practice mode**, without a real AI service. It's used for trying LAA out and for testing. In practice mode, everything still works (interviews, lessons, quizzes, the tutor), but the writing is a simple stand-in rather than real teaching, and much of it starts with **[FAKE]**.

If you see [FAKE], you're in practice mode. Whoever runs your copy of LAA can switch it to a real AI service.

## When something goes wrong

LAA is careful never to lose your work. If something fails, you'll see a short message saying what happened. Usually you just try again:

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *"…couldn't reply just now. Your answer wasn't lost: send it again."* | Tap **Send** again. Your answer is still in the box. |
| *"We couldn't check this topic just now, so nothing was created."* | Wait a moment, then try your topic again. |
| *"The lesson didn't pass its checks, so it wasn't saved."* | Tap **Write lesson** again on the contents page. |
| *"Your tutor couldn't read your answer just now. Nothing was lost."* | Tap **Send to your tutor** again. |
| *"Can't reach the server."* | Check your internet connection, then try again. |

Nothing half-finished is ever saved: an answer is kept together with the reply to it, and a lesson is saved only once it's complete and checked.

## Coming in future releases

- **Parent controls and reader settings:**
  - child-safe mode and an age slider that sets the reading level;
  - topics to leave out;
  - whether lessons may include links;
  - whether the answer can be shown after a few tries;
  - a parent PIN.
- **Safety checks on everything** LAA writes and on what learners type.
- **An owner console** for the people who run LAA: choosing which AI service writes each part, seeing what it costs, and comparing services before switching.
