# Learn Anything Anywhere: the guide

**Learn Anything Anywhere** (LAA for short) writes you a textbook for whatever you want to learn, built around *why* you want to learn it.

Tell it what you're curious about, and it asks you a few questions about your goal. Then it writes short lessons, one at a time, each moving you a step closer to that goal. A personal tutor checks your practice and helps when you're stuck, without simply handing you the answer, so what you learn sticks.

LAA works in a web browser and on phones and tablets. Your textbooks are kept for you, so you can stop on one device and carry on from another.

> **What's new?** See the [release notes](release-notes.md) for what each release added.

## Who it's for

- **Learners** of any age who want to learn a skill, a subject or a hobby: bouldering, sourdough, chess, Spanish for a trip, a programming language.
- **Parents**, who will be able to set up safe, age-appropriate learning for their children. Parent controls arrive in a later release.
- **The people who run LAA**, who choose which AI service writes the lessons. Their console arrives in a later release.

## The ideas behind LAA

LAA follows a few simple ideas about how people really learn. You'll see them all through the guide.

- **Start with why.** A clear reason gives every lesson a direction. That's why every textbook begins with a short interview about your goal.
- **Small steps, just beyond what you can already do.** Each lesson teaches one thing you can use straight away, pitched to stretch you without losing you.
- **Recall beats rereading.** Answering from memory, as in each lesson's warm-up, is what moves knowledge into long-term memory.
- **Find it yourself.** Working out an answer, with a nudge when you need one, teaches far more than being told. Your tutor coaches; it doesn't do the work for you.
- **Trust your sources.** LAA never makes up a book, website or study. It only points to sources you've added yourself.
